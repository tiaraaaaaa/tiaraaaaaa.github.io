TaBot: Designing an AI-empowered Robotic Table to Promote Balanced Conversational Experiences

Collaborators: X - Group · Immersive Design Group · Shenzhen Art Museum (New Venue)

Exhibition Human-Robot Interaction AI

Overview

TaBot is a robotic table that can sense and interpret the subtle atmosphere of a conversation—including its duration, pace, tone of voice, word choice, and language patterns—and provide ambient cues to participants through non-verbal movements.

By responding physically to conversational dynamics, TaBot explores how an AI-empowered shared object can promote more balanced conversational experiences without interrupting the ongoing discussion.

The project was exhibited at Shenzhen Art Museum (New Venue).
