PinScope at MIT Media Lab

Collaborator: MIT Media Lab City Science Group

ICIF2026 UABB 2025

Overview

From October to November 2025, I was a visiting student at the MIT Media Lab, working with the City Science Group. During this visit, I participated in an intensive research exchange centered on a collaborative project titled “Three-dimensional Community Simulation Interface: Designing and Manufacturing RoboScope, a Pin-Array Display to Facilitate Understanding Through Iteration of a Community’s Common Vision.”

Our project, PinScope, was incubated through this MIT-led collaboration and benefited from in-depth discussions with MIT faculty and researchers on its technical, social, and urban implications. Building upon the foundational concepts of RoboScope, the work explored further applications of large-scale pin-array displays as spatial interfaces for community visioning, urban simulation, and iterative decision-making.

The project was led by Prof. Kent Larson (MIT Media Lab) in collaboration with Prof. Seungwoo Je (SUSTech School of Design) and supported by the MIT Greater China Fund for Innovation.
